Infirmary Service

"Genesis Women's and Child Care was founded on June 14, 2019 by Mrs.Magudapathi with a heartfelt mission — to provide quality, compassionate healthcare for human beings in the Pappanayakenpalayam community."

We focused on delivering personalized medical care in a welcoming and supportive environment. As our commitment and reputation grew, we expanded our services. On June 14 2018, we opened Genesis Royal Infirmary in Puliyakulam, further extending our reach and ability to serve more patients with advanced facilities and specialized care. Today, Genesis stands as a trusted name in human beings healthcare, blending medical expertise, modern technology, and a compassionate touch — ensuring every patient feels valued, respected, and cared for.
